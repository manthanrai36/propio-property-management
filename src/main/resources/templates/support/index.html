Knowledge Base

Navigate to Payments in your navigation sidebar. You will see all your active and previous invoices. Click the Pay Online button on any PENDING or OVERDUE invoice to access our secure simulated payment portal. Once completed, your invoice status updates to PAID and a Download Receipt button becomes available to download your PDF receipt.

Go to Service Requests and click New Request. Select your category (Plumbing, Electrical, HVAC, etc.), priority level, write a brief description, and optionally upload a photo. You can view real-time timeline progress and post comments directly on the ticket page. You will receive email and in-app notifications as our team updates the status.

Your executed residential lease agreement is accessible on both the My Leases page and the Documents repository. You can download the full signed PDF at any time. For tax filing or expense tracking, annual resident statements can be downloaded directly from your profile page.

Rent invoices are generated automatically at the start of each monthly billing cycle and are due on the designated lease payment date. If an invoice remains unpaid past its due date, the system automatically marks it OVERDUE and assesses the standard late fee surcharge configured by property management.

Visit Settings from the sidebar or click your profile menu in the top-right corner. You can upload an avatar photo, update your phone number, or select the Security & Password tab to safely change your password with BCrypt encryption.
